# Phase4 design system

A warm school annual with the confidence of a contemporary editorial publication. Real photographs, large expressive serif headlines, quiet captions, open space and well-defined rules.

- Surfaces: warm ivory `#f6f4ec`, paper `#fffcf5`, deep forest `#193c32`.
- Text: ink `#193c32`, muted `#586359`, white `#fffcf5` on forest.
- Accent: terracotta `#af432f`; soft sage `#dbe5d5`; chartreuse `#e2e9b8`.
- Display: locally hosted DM Serif Display, normal and italic; body: locally hosted Manrope 400–800.
- Type: display at most 96px; section titles 40–58px, body 15–18px. Serif italics carry selected emphasis. No decorative eyebrows.
- Width: 1280px maximum with fluid 24–64px gutters. Use 90–112px between major sections on desktop, 56–72px on mobile.
- Shapes: editorial photo rectangles, minimal corner radius. Pills only on small filters/tags. Rules organize award lists, statistics and resources.
- Images: preserve original aspect ratios in galleries and detailed pages; intentional crops only in overview covers. Supply small thumbnails and full images.
- Motion: first-view image reveal only; content remains visible without JavaScript. Respect reduced motion.
- Interaction: visibly underlined text links; offset keyboard focus ring; native dialog for gallery enlargement with focus return, captions and keyboard navigation. Filter controls expose pressed state and counts.
- Responsive: two-column hero becomes stacked, navigation becomes a disclosed mobile menu, card and gallery grids reduce from three to two to one. No horizontal clipping used to conceal overflow.
